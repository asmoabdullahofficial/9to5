# The Voice of the Conscious Soul

**ASMO ABDULLAH** is a dedicated Rapper and Lyricist from Bangladesh, currently based in Muscat, Oman. Focusing on powerful storytelling and authentic rap delivery, he brings a raw perspective to the modern hip-hop scene.

### My Journey
Starting my rap journey at the age of 17, I've always believed that music is more than just rhythm—it's a medium for truth. After a brief hiatus to reshape my vision, I returned with a mission to create "Conscious Rap." 

### Why Conscious Rap?
Every bar I write is a testament to my journey and my unwavering passion for the art of music. I believe in lyrics that reflect real-life struggles, ambitions (like the **9 to 5** grind), and deep human emotions (like **ICCHE**). 

> "I don't just write rhymes; I document reality."
