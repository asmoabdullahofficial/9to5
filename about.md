        <h1>The Voice of the Conscious Soul</h1>
        <p><strong>ASMO ABDULLAH</strong> is a dedicated Rapper and Lyricist from Bangladesh, currently based in Muscat, Oman. With a focus on powerful storytelling and authentic rap delivery, he brings a raw perspective to the modern hip-hop scene.</p>

        <h3>My Journey</h3>
        <p>Starting my rap journey at the age of 17, I've always believed that music is more than just rhythm — it's a medium for truth. After a brief hiatus to reshape my vision, I returned with a mission to create <strong>"Conscious Rap"</strong>.</p>

        <h3>Why Conscious Rap?</h3>
        <p>Every bar I write is a testament to my journey and my unwavering passion for the art of music. I believe in lyrics that reflect real-life struggles, ambitions (like the <strong>9 to 5</strong> grind), and deep human emotions (like <strong>ICCHE</strong>).</p>
